**KESTREL YARD** — a round-based tactical FPS in three.js, Counter-Strike in spirit and original in every asset: your own map (a dusk freight yard with two plant sites), your own teams (the Ravens attack, the Wardens hold), your own guns. 4v4 against bots with waypoint routing, vision cones and hearing; an economy that pays for kills and rounds and a buy menu for rifles, a DMR, a shotgun, frags, flashes and vests; a charge to plant and defuse; gunplay where spread blooms on the move and headshots pay — stopping to aim is the whole game. The simulation runs on a fixed 60 Hz step, apart from rendering.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080
```

## Hand it in

```sh
node main.ts    # copies game/ and screenshots/ to /output and writes dossier.json
```
